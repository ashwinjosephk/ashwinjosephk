### 🧭 About Me

I'm a Research Associate at **Fraunhofer FHR** (High Frequency Physics & Radar Techniques), working on radar perception, sensor fusion, electromagnetic digital-twin simulation, and mmWave radar signal processing.

- 🎓 Dual M.Sc. — **Robotic Systems Engineering** (RWTH Aachen) & **Computer Science / AI** (CUSAT, Kerala)
- 🛰️ M.Sc. thesis at **DLR Neustrelitz**: Visual-LiDAR SLAM for autonomous inland waterway navigation — resulting paper submitted to **IEEE OJ-ITS** on semantic 3D mapping
- 🏭 Previously: Computer Vision & AI at Ignitarium/Neurealm and Sony India — edge AI deployment on Qualcomm Snapdragon & NVIDIA hardware
- 🇮🇳 Originally from Kerala, India

### 🎯 Currently

- 🔄 Pivoting toward **VLA / VLM roles** — following a structured roadmap anchored on the ETH Zürich robot learning course
- ⚙️ Building out modern **C++** for robotics engineering, alongside my Python/PyTorch background
- 📡 Alumnus of the Fraunhofer FHR International Radar Summer School 2026 (SAR imaging, SSA, bistatic/passive radar)

### 🔬 Featured Work

| Project | Description |
|---|---|
| **Warehouse Radar Digital Twin** | End-to-end pipeline for MIMO forklift radar (60 GHz) simulation: Isaac Sim USD export → Blender scripting → Mitsuba XML → NVIDIA Sionna RT ray tracing. Targeted for publication. |
| **Isaac Sim Sensor Simulation** | Drone simulation with LiDAR, camera, and radar sensors via RTX Radar & OmniGraph, integrated with ROS2 Humble. |
| **Visual-LiDAR SLAM (M.Sc. Thesis)** | Semantic 3D mapping for autonomous inland waterway navigation, DLR Neustrelitz — paper submitted to IEEE OJ-ITS. |
| **Synthetic Data Pipeline** | BlenderProc-based synthetic dataset generation for drink detection — U-Net/ResNet50 semantic segmentation, COCO annotations, domain randomization. |
